# CarePilot AI — Prototype v7.2

CarePilot AI keeps breast cancer survivors, their caregivers and the care team connected after active treatment. It turns each survivor's care plan into tracked actions, engages patients and caregivers on WhatsApp in their own language, and lets an AI agent handle routine follow-up coordination, while coordinators handle exceptions and oncologists keep every clinical decision.

## Important

This is a prototype. All patients, records, documents and messages are synthetic; uploads and hospital feeds are mocked. CarePilot organises, reminds, tracks and routes. It never diagnoses, prescribes, scores clinical risk, interprets results or advises patients — people decide. The breast cancer follow-up plan shown is illustrative, pending clinical sign-off.
